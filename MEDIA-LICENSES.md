# Media and study-content licences

The repository’s MIT licence applies to application source code. It does not override the licences of photographs, third-party illustrations, fonts or other assets.

## Wikimedia Commons assets

For each displayed asset, `content/media.json` records its original file-page URL, creator, licence, licence URL, attribution, source caption, source revision, local path and checksum. The app displays a compact source credit and an image-information dialog linking to the full source record.

Local specimens are resized/converted WebP derivatives. Attribution states that transformation. The individual source licence continues to apply, including any attribution and share-alike requirements. The importer accepts CC BY/CC BY-SA (including historical 2.0/2.5/3.0 versions), CC0 and explicit public-domain files. It rejects noncommercial, no-derivatives, unspecified and restricted-source records rather than assuming all educational images are reusable.

Recorded source metadata is evidence, not a legal guarantee about the uploader. Source-page descriptions and licence links are retained for review, correction and removal when needed. Research contact sheets are internal collection/QA aids whose constituent images retain their individual licences.

Roboto, JetBrains Mono and Material Symbols Outlined are distributed through their `@fontsource` packages. Their package copyright/licence notices are retained in installed dependencies. They are self-hosted so the installed atlas can render offline.
