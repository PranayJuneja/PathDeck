## Final curriculum audit — 2026-11-04

207 original educational entries across 19 systems: 52 general-pathology, 130 systemic-pathology and 25 hematopathology entries. Each has a definition, mechanism, gross and microscopic observations, clinical context, exam caution, differential, image queries and references. Empty stain arrays mean that no useful routine ancillary stain was specified; they are intentional.

The survey covers core MBBS general mechanisms, common systemic disease, hematology and transfusion, immune injury and immunodeficiency, basic genetics, nutrition and selected environmental injury. It is not a rare-disease encyclopedia or a formal mapping to every institution-specific competency. No claim of independent educator or pathologist certification is made. Original prose is not copied from the cited pages.

### Important source corrections

- Removed empty Libre pages for megaloblastic/aplastic anemia, thalassemia, hereditary spherocytosis, G6PD deficiency, autoimmune hemolysis and ALL. Replaced them with NIH, NCI, MedlinePlus or actual Utah teaching pages.
- Replaced empty dysplasia, foreign-body granuloma, teratoma, endometrioid carcinoma and thyroid-adenoma targets with substantive pages.
- Silicosis now cites Utah INFL059, not the misleading Libre redirect to hypersensitivity pneumonitis. Leprosy now cites CDC clinical overview, not only a broad bacterial page.
- Glioblastoma notes retain current IDH-wildtype terminology. The older Libre page is a morphology reference; WHO CNS5 official book metadata was verified by the parent/QA agent, but its full classification text could not be fetched. This full-text verification limit remains explicit rather than regressing to obsolete IDH-mutant glioblastoma terminology.
- Added p57 complete/partial/nonmolar limitations, CML t(9;22) and Burkitt MYC translocation context.
- General mechanisms cite actual OpenStax chapters. Some gross/microscopic fields explain that a clinical syndrome has no unique tissue lesion, instead of inventing a diagnostic appearance.

### Retrieval limits

- NCBI Bookshelf and several PubMed/PMC full-text routes were blocked or returned unusable footer/CAPTCHA content. They were not represented as fully inspected source text.
- MSD/Merck anemia pages returned empty fallback extraction. NHS vitamin pages had connection resets; NIH ODS fact-sheet calls timed out. These failed pages were not used as the sole final references.
- Historical failed/empty Libre attempts above are retained as the research trail; the final cited-source index and topics.json contain corrected targets.
- Utah WebPath material is all rights reserved: links support verification only; no text or images were copied into the dataset. Libre text licensing is BY-NC-SA3.0; original factual summaries are newly written, and image licensing is handled independently per asset.

### Search summary

- Commands: webcmd --version; webcmd web fetch --url <official or educational source> --max-chars <limit>; bounded parallel invocations of the same fetch command; official web open fallback for CDC, WHO and selected references after unreliable extraction.
- Browser fetch fallback: none.
- Sources fetched: linked in the successful retrieval batches and current cited-source index above. Peer QA independently verified CDC hemostasis, Lifeblood transfusion and several Utah sources; WHO CNS5 metadata verification is explicitly distinguished from full-text review.
