# Path Deck content QA — independent sampled review

Reviewed on 2026-11-04. This is an implementation QA ledger, not a claim of specialist editorial certification or exhaustive competency mapping.

## Scope and evidence limits

- Initial whole-catalog coverage scan: 142 topics; targeted microscopy/stain/exam-tip review: 30 entries across organ systems.
- The stable final catalog contains 207 topics across 19 systems; newly added topics are not automatically covered by the 30-topic review.
- Each statement below identifies the specific claim actually checked. A source supporting a characteristic stain or architectural pattern does not establish that every sentence, differential, clinical association or image for that topic was independently verified.
- No major contradiction in the sampled core morphology was confirmed. The most actionable findings were incomplete source support, obsolete terminology within some source pages, and syllabus gaps rather than a need to rewrite otherwise cautious notes.
- No patient-specific diagnosis, treatment or dosing recommendations were added.

## Corrections and follow-up sent to the curriculum agent

1. **Megaloblastic anemia reference**: the supplied Libre Pathology page returned an empty article. Replace it with [University of Utah WebPath HEME083](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/HEMEHTML/HEME083.html), which actually describes macro-ovalocytes and the B12/folate biochemical distinction, and [HEMEIDX](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/HEMEHTML/HEMEIDX.html), which links hypersegmented-neutrophil specimens. This is a source-support defect, not a demonstrated error in the current smear description.
2. **Leprosy reference**: the Libre Pathology URL resolves to a broad bacterial-infections article and does not clearly substantiate the whole disease spectrum. [CDC clinical overview](https://www.cdc.gov/leprosy/hcp/clinical-overview/index.html) directly supports the organisms, immune-dependent clinical spectrum, skin and peripheral-nerve involvement. Use it alongside a verified tissue/stain reference.
3. **Glioblastoma nomenclature reference**: the current notes correctly use an integrated IDH-wildtype framing, but the fetched Libre Pathology source still uses the older “IDH-mutant glioblastoma” category. Do not regress the topic text to match that page. The [2021 WHO summary](https://academic.oup.com/neuro-oncology/article/23/8/1231/6311214) was identified but inaccessible through the fetch tools; a current accessible primary classification reference remains desirable. The fetched NCI adult-brain PDQ page also contained legacy classification sections, so it was not treated as resolving this issue.
4. **Hydatidiform mole exam detail**: the general p57 statement is not false, but the high-yield distinction should be explicit: most complete moles lack nuclear p57 in villous stromal cells/cytotrophoblast; partial and nonmolar gestations generally retain it. p57 alone does not separate partial mole from nonmolar abortion. [Fetched source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Hydatidiform_mole).
5. **Classic molecular associations**: add Philadelphia t(9;22)/BCR::ABL1 to CML and MYC-immunoglobulin rearrangement, commonly t(8;14), to Burkitt lymphoma if the exam notes aim to cover the usual MBBS associations. Current generalized molecular statements are not contradicted; this is a high-yield completeness improvement. [CML](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Chronic_myeloid_leukemia), [Burkitt](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Burkitt_lymphoma).

## Thirty-topic targeted review

1. **Amyloidosis** — Supported: extracellular deposits, nonbranching fibrils and Congo-red polarized birefringence. The notes appropriately separate identifying amyloid from typing the precursor. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Amyloidosis).
2. **Myocardial infarction** — Supported: ischemic coronary occlusion and coagulative necrosis. Healing granulation tissue is also represented in the fetched university inflammation index. The full neutrophil/macrophage time course and reperfusion timing caveat were not independently re-derived from a timed clinical series. [University infarction example](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/ATHHTML/ATH047.html), [healing examples](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/INFLHTML/INFLIDX.html).
3. **Small cell lung carcinoma** — Supported: scant cytoplasm, stippled chromatin/nuclear molding and neuroendocrine-marker use. The source supports a differential with lymphoid lesions. INSM1-specific performance and every individual listed marker were not independently assessed in a current validation paper. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Small_cell_carcinoma).
4. **Malignant mesothelioma** — Supported: invasive epithelioid/spindle patterns, mesothelial-marker panels and BAP1 loss as an ancillary finding. “A positive mesothelial marker alone does not establish malignancy” is an appropriate distinction. Avoid importing obsolete source statements about never diagnosing mesothelioma in situ. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Malignant_mesothelioma).
5. **Celiac disease** — Supported: intraepithelial lymphocytosis, villous abnormalities and important infectious/other enteropathy mimics. The CD3 adjunct and all clinical qualifiers were not separately validated beyond the article-level review. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Celiac_disease).
6. **Crohn disease** — Supported: patchy/transmural disease concepts and the limited specificity/sensitivity of granulomas. Current cautions about the limitations of a mucosal biopsy are sensible. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Crohn_disease).
7. **Ulcerative colitis** — Supported: crypt distortion, basal plasmacytosis, active cryptitis/crypt abscesses and classically continuous colorectal distribution. The fetched source itself notes rectal sparing/interpretative exceptions; the current notes avoid an absolute distribution rule. The selected-case CMV ancillary statement was not separately guideline-verified. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Ulcerative_colitis).
8. **Alcohol-associated hepatitis** — Supported: steatosis, ballooning, Mallory–Denk bodies, neutrophils and perisinusoidal fibrosis. The notes correctly avoid claiming Mallory–Denk bodies are alcohol-specific. [More directly supporting source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Steatohepatitis).
9. **Hepatocellular carcinoma** — Supported: thickened trabeculae, reticulin disruption, bile production and possible occurrence without cirrhosis. HepPar-1 support was verified; Arginase-1-specific marker evidence was not independently reviewed. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Hepatocellular_carcinoma).
10. **Minimal change disease** — Supported: little light-microscopic change, podocyte foot-process effacement and the need to distinguish undersampled FSGS. The absence of a conspicuous lesion does not imply normal renal function. No therapeutic-response guarantee was imported from the source. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Nephrotic_syndrome).
11. **Focal segmental glomerulosclerosis** — Supported: segmental scarring in a subset of glomeruli and differences between primary/adaptive forms. Current “focal” and “segmental” definitions avoid the misleading idea that a rigid proportion of all glomeruli must be sclerosed. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Nephrotic_syndrome).
12. **Infection-related glomerulonephritis** — Supported: exudative/neutrophilic pattern, granular immune deposits and subepithelial humps. The notes correctly avoid equating a hump with a unique etiology. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Post-infectious_glomerulonephritis).
13. **Iron-deficiency anemia** — Supported: small pale red cells and anisopoikilocytosis. The notes appropriately treat microcytosis as a pattern. Perls stain use was not revalidated from a specific marrow-methods source in this pass. [University source](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/HEMEHTML/HEME008.html).
14. **Megaloblastic anemia** — Supported: macro-ovalocytes and hypersegmented-neutrophil examples. B12/folate laboratory distinctions are present in the university source. Marrow nuclear/cytoplasmic asynchrony was not separately revalidated from a marrow monograph. Replace the empty reference as above. [Specific source](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/HEMEHTML/HEME083.html), [index](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/HEMEHTML/HEMEIDX.html).
15. **Acute myeloid leukemia** — Supported: Auer rods in suitable forms, MPO support for myeloid differentiation and genetic classification. The notes correctly avoid imposing one universal blast cutoff. Individual WHO/ICC genetic thresholds were not audited or added. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Acute_myeloid_leukemia).
16. **Chronic myeloid leukemia** — Supported: BCR::ABL1/Philadelphia association, granulocytes at multiple maturation stages and basophilia. The small hypolobated-megakaryocyte detail was not independently checked in a marrow-specific source. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Chronic_myeloid_leukemia).
17. **Classical Hodgkin lymphoma** — Supported: Reed–Sternberg cells, lacunar forms, CD30/CD15 and relatively weak PAX5. The distinction between a suggestive cell and a diagnosis supported by architecture/phenotype is appropriate. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Hodgkin_lymphoma).
18. **Burkitt lymphoma** — Supported: high turnover, tingible-body macrophages, CD10/BCL6, high Ki-67 and MYC rearrangement. The notes correctly avoid treating starry-sky morphology as specific. Do not import an assertion that every case must be EBV-positive. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Burkitt_lymphoma).
19. **Hydatidiform mole** — Supported: abnormal hydropic villi, type-dependent morphology, p57/genotyping use. Add the explicit p57 limitation described above. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Hydatidiform_mole).
20. **Choriocarcinoma** — Supported: malignant cytotrophoblast/syncytiotrophoblast, hemorrhage/necrosis and lack of chorionic villi in a pure lesion, contrasting with invasive mole. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Hydatidiform_mole).
21. **Invasive lobular carcinoma** — Supported: discohesive single-file growth and E-cadherin loss as a classic pattern. p120's subcellular pattern and bilateral/multifocal frequency were not independently quantified in this pass. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Invasive_lobular_carcinoma).
22. **Thyroid follicular adenoma** — Supported: the distinction from follicular carcinoma depends on excluding diagnostic invasion; fine-needle cytology cannot establish the relevant capsular/vascular boundary. [Paired differential source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Follicular_thyroid_carcinoma).
23. **Follicular thyroid carcinoma** — Supported: follicular growth with diagnostic capsular or vascular invasion, and limitations of FNA. No numerical vascular-invasion prognostic cutoff was added. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Follicular_thyroid_carcinoma).
24. **Medullary thyroid carcinoma** — Supported: C-cell origin, calcitonin, neuroendocrine morphology and stromal amyloid/Congo red. Source discussion distinguishes inherited MEN-associated disease from sporadic cases. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Medullary_thyroid_carcinoma).
25. **Pheochromocytoma** — Supported: adrenal medullary origin, zellballen architecture, chromogranin/synaptophysin in chief cells and S100 in sustentacular cells. The notes avoid certifying benign behavior from bland morphology. SOX10 was not independently checked from a marker-validation paper. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Pheochromocytoma).
26. **Ewing sarcoma** — Supported: a small-round-cell pattern, supportive but nonspecific CD99, and a fusion-associated diagnosis; the fetched source gives EWSR1/FLI1 and other partners. The current caution about CD99 alone is useful. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Ewing_sarcoma).
27. **Glioblastoma** — Supported: infiltrating atypical glial cells, necrosis/pseudopalisading and microvascular proliferation, with GFAP support. The current integrated molecular wording is more careful than the legacy source, but current WHO naming was not fully verified through an accessible primary classification page. Source-upgrade action remains as above. [Morphology source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Glioblastoma).
28. **Leprosy** — Supported from CDC: causative organisms, immune-dependent spectrum, sensory/nerve involvement and use of tissue diagnosis with acid-fast staining. The exact Fite-method statement and foamy macrophage detail were not independently established from a primary histology protocol in this pass. [CDC source](https://www.cdc.gov/leprosy/hcp/clinical-overview/index.html).
29. **Aspergillosis** — Supported: relatively uniform septate hyphae with acute-angle branching, fungal stains and angioinvasive examples. The current note correctly says morphology is not sufficient for definitive species assignment. [Source](https://librepathology.org/wiki/Aspergillosis).
30. **Tuberculosis** — Supported at a general teaching level: necrotizing granulomatous morphology and giant-cell forms in the university atlas. The negative-stain-does-not-exclude statement was not independently rechecked against a TB microbiology guideline during this pass. [University inflammation source](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/INFLHTML/INFLIDX.html).

## Coverage scan: originally missing high-yield areas

These were messages to the curriculum agent based on the initial 142-entry snapshot, not an assertion that all remain absent after expansion.

- General pathology: atrophy/hypertrophy/hyperplasia, edema/congestion, shock/sepsis, neoplasia principles/carcinogenesis, major autoimmunity.
- Respiratory: asthma, chronic bronchitis and ARDS/diffuse alveolar damage.
- Renal: crescentic GN, lupus nephritis, polycystic kidney disease, hydronephrosis and stones.
- Hematology/clinical pathology: hemophilia, von Willebrand disease, transfusion reactions, anemia of chronic disease; basic blood-bank concepts remain a distinct coverage domain from tumor microscopy.
- Endocrine: multinodular goiter, pituitary adenoma and adrenal cortical disorders.
- Additional practical entities: phyllodes tumour, chronic pancreatitis, rickets/osteomalacia/osteoporosis, malaria and amoebiasis.
- Genetics, nutrition and environmental disease were thin at the initial scan. New entries are being added separately and should be counted from the latest catalog before any final coverage statement.
- A formal checklist against every NMC competency or each university's practical list was not performed. This review verifies representative content and flags broad omissions, not a guarantee of complete exam coverage.

## Additional primary references delivered for expansion

- [CDC hemophilia](https://www.cdc.gov/hemophilia/about/index.html): A versus B factor deficiency, inherited forms, bleeding presentation and factor assays.
- [CDC VWD](https://www.cdc.gov/von-willebrand/about/index.html): VWF quantity/function, platelet adhesion, broad type distinctions, mucosal bleeding and factor VIII relationship.
- [NHLBI bleeding-disorder types](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/bleeding-disorders/types): cross-checks factor VIII/IX/XI-related disorders.
- [Lifeblood acute hemolytic reactions](https://www.lifeblood.com.au/health-professionals/clinical-practice/adverse-events/acute-haemolytic-reactions): immune/nonimmune distinction, ABO/non-ABO causes and diagnostic work-up.
- [Lifeblood TRALI](https://www.lifeblood.com.au/health-professionals/clinical-practice/adverse-events/TRALI): noncardiogenic permeability injury, clinical diagnosis, typical onset within six hours. Antibody demonstration is not required for diagnosis.
- [Lifeblood TACO](https://www.lifeblood.com.au/health-professionals/clinical-practice/adverse-events/TACO): hydrostatic overload, cardiac/fluid-balance evidence and surveillance window up to twelve hours; avoid treating one vital-sign pattern as mandatory in every patient.
- [CDC carbon monoxide](https://www.cdc.gov/carbon-monoxide/hcp/clinical-guidance/index.html): impaired oxygen delivery and cellular use, CO-oximetry and limitations of conventional pulse oximetry. No treatment dose was copied into the curriculum.
- [WHO lead poisoning](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/lead-poisoning-and-health): neurodevelopmental, renal and hematologic effects and blood-lead measurement; [Utah HEME012](https://webpath.med.utah.edu/HEMEHTML/HEME012.html) supports nonspecific basophilic stippling. Specific heme-enzyme inhibition was not established from the fetched sources in this pass.

## Search summary

- Commands: `webcmd web fetch`, focused text extraction from fetched pages, and `web.run` direct opens/clicks when the CLI fetch failed or returned unusable content.
- Sources: exact successful links are attached to each checked claim above. University, NIH/CDC, WHO and national blood-service sources were preferred where accessible; Libre Pathology was used as a specialist secondary source with explicit caution about outdated sections.
- Browser fetch fallback: none. Headless Chrome used for earlier SVG layout QA is unrelated to medical-source verification.
- Failures/gaps: some CDC requests returned `ECONNRESET` in Webcmd and were subsequently readable in the web tool; old ATSDR lead and standalone NHLBI VWD URLs returned 404; NCBI Bookshelf/OUP requests were blocked; the NCI adult-brain PDQ page contained legacy classification text and did not resolve current WHO naming.

## Four final targeted diagrams (22 originals total)

The final source audit rejected normal marrow anatomy as evidence for aplastic anemia and found four additional conditions without suitable disease-specific external visuals. Four original mechanisms were added without relabeling generic photographs:

- **Obesity/type 2 diabetes:** [NIDDK insulin resistance](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/prediabetes-insulin-resistance), [NIDDK type 2 diabetes](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diabetes/overview/what-is-diabetes/type-2-diabetes), and [NHLBI metabolic syndrome causes](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/metabolic-syndrome/causes) support adipose/free-fatty-acid/inflammatory signals, reduced tissue response, compensatory secretion and inadequate later beta-cell output. The diagram explicitly states obesity is neither necessary nor sufficient, and does not present autoimmune beta-cell loss as typical type 2 diabetes.
- **Chronic bronchitis:** the [original Reid publication record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14437095/) and [Reid mucus review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5370700/) establish measurement of gland thickness relative to wall from epithelial basement membrane to cartilage/perichondrium. The original schematic uses a large cartilaginous bronchus, with mucus and enlarged glands; it does not put submucosal glands or a Reid measurement in a bronchiole. The simplified geometry is explicitly not a diagnostic-scale measurement. Utah WebPath supplies a disease-specific specimen link, but the readable Reid text provides the measurement evidence.
- **Vitamin A:** [NIH ODS vitamin A](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminA-HealthProfessional/) directly supports rhodopsin-dependent dim-light responses and normal ocular epithelial maintenance. Parallel panels distinguish early night blindness from ocular surface injury/xerophthalmia, with severe corneal disease threatening vision. It does not prescribe supplements or equate xerophthalmia solely with night blindness.
- **Aplastic anemia:** [NHLBI](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/anemia/aplastic-anemia) and [Merck Professional](https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/hematology/anemias-caused-by-deficient-erythropoiesis/aplastic-anemia) support stem-cell injury, hypocellular marrow and multilinage cytopenias. An original cellular-versus-fat-rich hypocellular marrow comparison is clearly schematic. Reduced red cells, white cells and platelets are labeled, and pure red-cell aplasia is explicitly distinguished. No numeric severity threshold, treatment or drug dose is included.

Final owned assets: 22 original SVGs, associated with 71 distinct exact IDs from 207 stable topics. All 22 pass XML/title/description/viewBox checks. The final four were rendered and visually inspected; text spills in initial drafts were corrected. Final Chromium checks found zero outside-canvas text boxes in all four. The other 18 had passed the same check. Temporary QA renders were removed. This does not expand the 30-topic independent note review into a claim that all 207 topic texts were medically audited.


## Final false-match replacements: scurvy and ITP (24 originals)

The final visual source audit rejected historical ship-layout images for scurvy and a drug chemical structure for ITP. Neither was retained as a pathological illustration. Two precise original mechanisms replace those forced matches:

- **Scurvy:** [NIH ODS vitamin C](https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminC-HealthProfessional/) supports connective-tissue weakness, capillary fragility and wound healing. The canonical page initially returned 403, but the same NIH page with its indexed query suffix was readable. [Primary procollagen hydroxylation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1720597/) and [UMass teaching notes](https://www.umassmed.edu/globalassets/office-of-undergraduate-medical-education-media/documents/protein_structure_notes_2016.pdf) support the proline/lysine hydroxylation and stability mechanism. The illustration highlights this educational pathway rather than claiming every scurvy manifestation is explained by one reaction; no nutrient dose or treatment was added.
- **Immune thrombocytopenia:** [NHLBI ITP](https://www.nhlbi.nih.gov/health/immune-thrombocytopenia), [ASH spleen/clearance review](https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2017-09-742353), and [ASH consensus](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2019000812) support immune platelet clearance, splenic macrophages, impaired production and the usual isolated thrombocytopenia pattern. The diagram explicitly describes an illustrative antibody pathway with other immune mechanisms also participating. It distinguishes this count disorder from thrombotic microangiopathy and VWF dysfunction, without implying antibody testing is a universally diagnostic test.

Both new diagrams were rendered and visually inspected; an initial malformed collagen path was repaired and re-rendered. Chromium text checks found no outside-canvas text. All 24 originals parse, have accessible title/description and exact topic-ID references; metadata now covers 73 distinct related topics. Temporary rendering files were removed. This is a final asset-quality correction, not an extension of the independent 30-topic note review.
